Know the Score

Privacy Policy

Short version: The free version of Know the Score shows ads from Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners collect device and ad data (such as your device's advertising identifier, if you allow it, and your approximate location) to show and measure those ads. You can limit this in the App, in iOS Settings, or remove ads completely with the ad-free subscription. If you're ad-free, the App doesn't start Google's ad software at all. Your games and player names stay on your device and in your own private iCloud; we never receive them. Photos, voice and nearby sharing are handled on your device and are never sent over the internet. We don't sell your personal information for money, but personalized advertising may count as "sharing" or a "sale" under some US state laws, and you can opt out (see below).

Who We Are

Know the Score ("the App", formerly "5 Crowns Scorekeeper") is a score-tracking app for many tabletop card, dice and domino games. It is published by Randy Cox, an individual developer based in the United States ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of data protection law, Randy Cox is the controller of personal data processed through the App.

Privacy contact: temples-rumpus8l@icloud.com

The App is independent. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the makers or owners of any game it can score. This policy explains what information is collected through the App, why, who receives it, and your choices and rights.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows banner, full-screen (interstitial) and app-open ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (and, for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). Google may share data with its certified ad-technology partners, which are listed in the consent form and at Google's list of ad technology providers.

Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for all ages (Google's "General" content rating).

What Google's Ad Software May Collect (free version only)

  • Identifiers: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking; an app-specific vendor identifier (IDFV); and your IP address
  • Approximate location (such as country, region or city), derived from your IP address. Not precise GPS location.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, language and similar technical details
  • Usage and advertising data: which ads were shown, ad impressions and taps, and how you interact with the App's ads
  • Diagnostics: crash, performance and other diagnostic data about the ad software

Google uses this data to serve, personalize (where you allow it) and measure ads, for analytics, to limit how often you see an ad, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google may combine it with other data it holds as described in its own privacy policy. We do not receive your IDFA, IP address or location; we only see aggregated ad reports from Google.

How to Opt Out or Manage Ad Preferences

  • In-app: Open Settings → Privacy & Ads → "Manage Ad Preferences" to review or change your choices at any time. This option appears where privacy choices are required, including the EEA, UK and US states with applicable privacy laws.
  • Tracking: Decline Apple's tracking prompt, or later go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn it off for Know the Score (or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track").
  • Google's controls: Visit My Ad Center to manage personalized ads for your Google Account.
  • Go ad-free: Subscribe to remove all ads (see "Ad-Free Subscription" below).
Google's collection and use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent and App Tracking Transparency

In the free version, when you first open the App you may see Google's consent form (where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and UK) and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The App asks Google for ads only when your consent choices allow it.

  • If you allow tracking, Google may use your IDFA to show more relevant, personalized ads.
  • If you decline tracking, you still see ads and Google does not receive your IDFA. Google may still receive the other information listed above (such as IP address and device information) to serve non-personalized ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Ad-Free Subscription & Purchases

The App offers an optional ad-free subscription through the Apple App Store ($0.99/month or $9.99/year in the US; prices may vary by region and are shown in the App Store before you buy). If you used the App before version 2.0, it stays ad-free for you at no charge.

When you're ad-free, the App does not show the tracking prompt or Google's consent form and does not start Google's ad software, so no ad requests are made and no data is sent to Google through the App. If you subscribe while the App is open, ads stop right away, and from the next time you open the App the ad software isn't started at all. Google's ad software remains part of the App's code but stays inactive. If a subscription ends, the free version's ads (and the consent and tracking prompts) return.

All payments are processed by Apple. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment or billing information; the App only learns from Apple whether you have an active subscription. Subscriptions are governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement and can be managed in your Apple Account settings.

Game Data Storage & iCloud Sync

Player names, scores, game history, saved player groups and your game in progress are stored on your device. We never receive them. Copies leave your device only when you choose: iCloud sync (below), sharing to nearby phones, showing the game on a TV, the Lock Screen scoreboard, or sending a result with the share sheet.

Player names you enter may be other people's names. Please enter only names your fellow players are happy to have recorded (nicknames work fine).

If you are signed into iCloud, the App uses Apple CloudKit to sync your finished-game history (game, options, player names, round scores, winners and times) across your own devices. This means:

  • It is stored in your private iCloud account, not on our servers
  • We have no access to your iCloud data
  • Apple encrypts it in transit and at rest
  • You can stop syncing by turning off iCloud for Know the Score in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud (look under the apps using iCloud; you may need to tap "See All")
Apple's handling of iCloud and CloudKit data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera (Counting Domino Dots from a Photo)

In domino games you can count the dots on leftover tiles from a photo. The photo is analyzed on your device. It is never uploaded, never sent to us or anyone else, and is not saved by the App; it is discarded when you close the counter. You can choose a photo from your library instead, in which case the App receives only the one photo you pick.

Camera access is optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings → Know the Score.

Microphone & Speech Recognition (Voice Score Entry)

You can say a round's scores instead of typing them. The App uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device-only mode: audio is never sent to Apple's servers or ours, and it is not recorded or stored. If your device can't recognize speech on-device, voice entry is simply unavailable.

Microphone and speech access are optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings → Know the Score.

Local Network (Scoreboard on Nearby Phones)

If you turn on sharing, the App uses Apple's peer-to-peer networking over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth to send the current game (game name, round, player names and scores) directly to nearby phones. The connection is encrypted, there is no server, and nothing goes over the internet. While sharing is on, any nearby phone running Know the Score can see that a game is being shared and watch it without your approval, so turn sharing off when you're done (it also stops when you leave the game). Nearby phones also see a device name, which is usually just "iPhone".

Lock Screen, TV Scoreboard, Sharing & How-to-Play Videos

  • Live Activities (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island scoreboard) are updated on your device only; there is no push server. They show player names and scores to anyone who can see your screen. You can turn them off in iOS Settings → Know the Score.
  • The TV scoreboard shows the game on a screen you connect with AirPlay or a cable.
  • A result image is created only when you tap Share, and goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet.
  • How-to-play videos are drawn and played by the App on your device; nothing is streamed or tracked.

  • Apple — iCloud (CloudKit) sync to your private account, App Store purchases (StoreKit), and on-device speech recognition. Apple Privacy Policy

Deleting Your Data

  • Game history: In the App, open Settings → Privacy & Ads → Delete All Game History. This removes every finished game and its stats from this device and from your iCloud, along with any backups kept from version 1.x of the App. Your game in progress and saved player groups are kept. If iCloud can't be reached, the App tells you, and you can try again when you're online.
  • Saved player groups: Press and hold a group on a game's setup screen and choose Delete Group.
  • Game in progress: End or discard it in the App.
  • Everything on this device: Delete the App. This removes all data the App stores on the device. Data in your iCloud stays until you delete it in the App (above) or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Storage) → Know the Score, if listed.
  • Ad data held by Google: We don't hold this data. You can reset your advertising identifier by turning tracking off, and manage Google's data through My Ad Center and Google's Privacy Policy. If you contact us, we'll help you reach Google.

Retention

Game data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it or delete the App. We don't keep copies. Google keeps ad data under its own retention policies. If you email us, we keep the messages for up to 24 months so we can show we handled your request, then delete them.
