Frequently Asked Questions

How many players can I add?
It depends on the game, from 1 up to 12 players. The setup screen shows the range for the game you pick.

Can I fix a score I entered wrong?
Yes. On the scoreboard, tap the round you want to change (look for the pencil) to edit that round's scores.

Does the app save my game if I close it?
Yes. Your game in progress is saved automatically, and the app offers to resume it the next time you open it. Finished games are kept in your history and stats, and sync to your other devices through your private iCloud if you're signed in.

How do I delete my game history?
Open Settings → Privacy & Ads → Delete All Game History. This removes every finished game and its stats from your device and your iCloud. Your game in progress and saved player groups stay. To delete a saved player group, press and hold it on a game's setup screen and choose Delete Group.

How do I remove ads?
Subscribe to the ad-free option in the app ($0.99/month or $9.99/year in the US; prices may vary by region). If you used the app before version 2.0, it's already ad-free for you. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Is this app affiliated with the games it scores?
No. Know the Score is an independent scorekeeping app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the makers or owners of any game it can score. Game names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe which games the app can keep score for.

Privacy

The free version shows Google ads, which use device data as described in our privacy policy. Ad-free users don't get Google's ad software at all. Your scores and player names stay on your device and in your private iCloud, and photos and voice are handled on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
